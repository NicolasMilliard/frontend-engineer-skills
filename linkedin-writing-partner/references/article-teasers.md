# Article Teasers

Use this mode when the LinkedIn post points to a longer article,
case study, project write-up, or technical essay.

The post must not become a compressed copy of the article.

Its job is to communicate:

1. enough context to understand the situation
2. one concrete incident or observation
3. the interesting tension it revealed
4. why the full story is worth reading

Default target: 120–180 words.

Reveal at most one major insight.

Do not include the complete:

- incident
- diagnosis
- implementation
- architecture
- trade-offs
- conclusion

Leave at least one meaningful technical question unresolved.

## Final test

Ask:

Could someone read this post instead of the article and understand
most of its technical reasoning?

If yes, reveal less.

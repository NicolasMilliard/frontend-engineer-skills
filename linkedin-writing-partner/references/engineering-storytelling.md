# Engineering Storytelling

## Finding the thesis

A topic is not a thesis.

Bad:

> Lessons from building a real-time frontend.

Better:

> WebSockets weren't the difficult part. Multiple competing sources
> of truth were.

Prefer a narrow claim that can be supported by the user's experience.

## Generate angles

Propose three meaningfully different angles.

For each provide:

### Thesis
The actual argument.

### Why it matters
Why an experienced engineer might care.

### Evidence
Which part of the user's experience supports it.

Angles must represent different interpretations, not different hooks.

## Narrative structures

Choose a structure based on the story.

Examples:

- Observation → Experience → Technical insight → Point of view
- Problem → Initial assumption → What broke → What changed
- Incident → Investigation → Architectural tension → Current opinion

Never force a generic LinkedIn formula.

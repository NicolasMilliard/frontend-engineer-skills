# Anti-patterns

## Generic LinkedIn formulas

Avoid:

- "Here's the thing"
- "The truth is"
- "Hot take"
- "Let that sink in"
- "The takeaway?"
- "X things I learned"

## Artificial contrast

Avoid constructions such as:

> It's not about X. It's about Y.

unless the contrast genuinely represents the argument.

## Manufactured significance

Do not turn ordinary engineering decisions into dramatic revelations.

Avoid vague adjectives unless justified:

- scalable
- robust
- elegant
- powerful
- seamless

## Engagement bait

Do not add:

- generic questions
- hashtags by default
- emojis by default
- motivational conclusions
- manufactured controversy

## Genericity test

Ask of every paragraph:

> Could hundreds of engineers have written this without changing
> anything?

If yes:

- make it specific
- attach it to an incident
- add a technical detail
- add a defensible opinion
- or remove it

# Interviewing

The interview exists to uncover a defensible point of view,
not to complete a questionnaire.

Look for:

- what actually happened
- initial assumptions
- surprising behavior
- technical constraints
- alternatives considered
- trade-offs
- mistakes
- consequences
- what changed in the engineer's thinking

Prefer:

> What was the first symptom that made you realize something was wrong?

Instead of:

> What did you learn?

Do not ask questions whose answers are already available.

Ask fewer, deeper questions.

If the material already contains enough concrete information to identify
a strong thesis, interviewing can be shortened.
